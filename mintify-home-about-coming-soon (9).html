About Mintify

Mintify started as a small experiment on Base and grew into a collection of 4,444 Wyrms, each assembled from hundreds of possible trait combinations. No two are exactly alike.

The collection is built from a shared library of hand-designed traits, carefully combined to create different colors, horns, wings, eyes, backgrounds, and gems. Every Wyrm has its own character and visual identity.

Mintify is designed to keep things simple: a collectible project built around unique characters, a growing world, and a community that gets to decide where the story goes next.

Lore

Lore

Long before Mintify was known, the Wyrms lived beyond the crystal valleys of Base — a hidden world where waterfalls carried glowing gems and every cave held traces of ancient energy.

The Wyrms were not born the same. Some emerged with dark scales, others with bright colors, strange horns, powerful wings, or mysterious gems embedded within them. Their appearance was shaped by the crystals they encountered as they grew.

For generations, the Wyrms lived quietly among the mountains, protecting the ancient crystals that powered their world. But one day, the largest crystal began to glow brighter than ever before.

A strange energy spread across the valley, awakening new traits within the Wyrms. Colors changed. Horns evolved. Wings appeared in unfamiliar forms. Even the oldest Wyrms could no longer predict what the next generation would look like.

The Wyrms began calling this event The Minting.

Now, thousands of unique Wyrms are preparing to leave their hidden world and enter a new one. No two are exactly alike, and every combination tells a small part of the story.

Some will become explorers. Some will become guardians. Some will simply cause chaos.

And somewhere among them, a few Wyrms may carry traits that have not been seen since the first crystal awakened.

The story has only just begun.

Overview

Mintify is a collection of 4,444 unique characters minted on Base. Each one is generated from a shared library of hand-drawn traits, then combined and reviewed before the public reveal.

The story

Mintify began as a small side project between a few builders who wanted to ship something on Base without over-promising. The name comes from a simple idea: we mint things, and we'd rather do a small collection well than a large one poorly.

About Mintify

Mintify started as a small experiment on Base and grew into a collection of 4,444 characters, each assembled from hundreds of possible trait combinations. No two are exactly alike.

The collection is generated programmatically from a shared trait library, then reviewed by hand before reveal. Mint access is offered first to whitelisted wallets, with the goal of keeping the initial mint fair and low-gas on Base.
